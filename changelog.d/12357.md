---
synopsis: Drop deprecation warnings for library-coverage and prefer-version flags
packages: [cabal-install]
prs: 12080
issues: 12357
---

The `library-coverage` and `prefer-version` flags of `cabal-install` were marked as deprecated with
reference to versions of the unrelated `.cabal` format spec, which is confusing, so we have dropped these
messages.
These flags are deprecated and we will likely re-introduce deprecation messages
when we have a better way to communicate that.
