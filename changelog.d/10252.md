---
synopsis: "Fix `curl: (56) Illegal or missing hexadecimal sequence in chunked-encoding` during `cabal upload`"
packages: [cabal-install]
prs: 12211
issues: 10252
---

- Removed the newline character from `--write-out` curl arguments when uploading packages. Newer versions of `curl` misinterpret this newline during chunked-encoding transfers, causing upload failures
- Adjusted `parseResponse` in `curlTransport` to extract the 3-digit status code directly from the end of the response rather than relying on line breaks
