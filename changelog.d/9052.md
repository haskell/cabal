---
synopsis: Warn when `-fprof-late` is passed as a GHC option with the default profiling detail
packages: [Cabal]
issues: 9052
prs: 0000
---

Passing `-fprof-late` through `ghc-options` while leaving `profiling-detail` at
its default means GHC adds the default cost centres on top of the late ones,
which adds overhead without giving a better profile. Cabal now warns about this
and suggests `--profiling-detail=late` (or `profiling-detail: late` in
`cabal.project`) instead.
