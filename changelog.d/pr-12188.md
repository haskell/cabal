---
synopsis: Tags are no longer recognised as a valid part of Version (x.y.z-tag)
packages: [Cabal-syntax]
prs: 12188
issues: 890
---

For many years a tag portion of a version as in `x.y.z-tag` was parsed
only to throw a warning. The tag was completely ignored afterwards and was not
even stored as a part of `Version`. This patch ends up the deprecation
period and stops parsing tags as a part of `Version`.

Consequently, a constructor for `PWTVersionTag` warning is gone from `data PWarnType`.
