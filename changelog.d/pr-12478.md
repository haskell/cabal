---
synopsis: Use Text instead of String in newtype Token and Token'
packages: [Cabal-syntax, cabal-install]
prs: 12478
---

Newtypes `Token` and `Token'` now wrap `Text` instead of `String`.
This entails switching the following record types to contain `Text` instead of `String` in their fields:
`InstalledPackageInfo`, `ProjectConfig`, `BuildTimeSettings`,
`SourceRepositoryPackage`, `SourceRepo`, `RemoteRepo`, `TestSuiteStanza` and `TestSuite`.
