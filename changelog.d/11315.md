---
synopsis: Support top-level conditional blocks in .cabal files
packages: [Cabal-syntax, cabal-install]
prs: 12442
issues: 11315
---

Since `cabal-version: 3.18`, top-level `if`/`elif`/`else` blocks in `.cabal`
files are accepted. Their fields are taken from *all* branches (the conditions
cannot be evaluated at parse time), so e.g. `data-files` listed inside a
top-level conditional are included in `cabal sdist`. Conditions are validated
for syntax only; branch conditions (including flag references) are not
evaluated. Before 3.18, top-level conditionals continue to be ignored with a
warning.
