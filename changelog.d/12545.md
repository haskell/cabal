---
synopsis: Faster configuring for packages with many `extra-source-files`
packages: [Cabal]
prs: 12617
issues: [12545]
---

When configuring, Cabal expanded every `extra-source-files`, `data-files` and
`extra-doc-files` glob, and listed the package directory, for checks whose
warnings it then discarded. These checks are now left to `cabal check` and
`sdist`. The remaining file checks are available as `checkPackageContentFiles`
from `Distribution.PackageDescription.Check`.
