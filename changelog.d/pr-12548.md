---
synopsis: Remove Distribution.Utils.MapAccum and Distribution.Compat.SysInfo
packages: [Cabal]
prs: 12548
---

Since the oldest supported GHC is now 9.6, we remove compatibility shims
`Distribution.Utils.MapAccum` (use `Data.Traversable.mapAccumM` instead)
and `Distribution.Compat.SysInfo` (use `System.Info.fullCompilerVersion` instead).
