---
synopsis: Support `--repl-no-load` with multiple home units
packages: [Cabal]
prs: 12585
issues: 11335
---

On supported GHC versions (>= 9.14.1), pass the option `-fno-load-initial-targets` to the final GHCi invocation.
This fixes support of the `--repl-no-load` flag when `--enable-multi-repl` is given.
Even for single home unit sessions, this makes GHCi more predictable.

For unsupported GHC versions, the old behaviour is maintained.
